How to play and controls

Catch within 4 seconds of your last catch to build a combo, up to ×5. If a charge hits you, the last light in your trail drifts away. Look out for rare Moodlings with sparkles: they fill new pages of the Mood Book.

Free Mode is endless: every catch adds 2 seconds, getting hit costs 3, and more feelings appear every 20 seconds. How long can you last?
