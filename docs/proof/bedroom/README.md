# Bedroom (coming-home scene)

- `bedroom-original.webp`: the bedroom design made by our team member.
- `bedroom-edited.webp`: our edit for the game. We removed the Moodlings hidden in the furniture, the scratch marks, the motion lines and the cat at the window, because in the story the feelings have left the room and Shwe is out at night.
- In the game (`game/assets/home/`) the room starts grey (`room-grey.webp`). Each feeling the player brings home gives the colour back to one part of the room (`room.webp`): bed, curtains, photos, rug and books, toys, desk, lights, plants, and the night sky.
- Thiri and Shwe are drawn in code on top of the picture.
- The room picture was upscaled with Real-ESRGAN (anime model) and given a calm night colour grade (soft glow on the lamps and lights, gentle lavender shadows). The desk papers, school bag pocket and the plush behind the bunny were repainted where the hidden Moodlings had been.
- Thiri sleeping in the bed (six poses, from tearful to awake) is cut from the team's painted reference of this same room, in which she sleeps on the bed with Shwe. She sits exactly where she lies in that picture. Claude removed the tears for the later poses, added a small smile, and painted open eyes for the last one; the grey versions are colour-matched to `room-grey.webp`.
