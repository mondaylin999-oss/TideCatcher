# Bedroom (coming-home scene)

- `bedroom-original.webp`: the bedroom design made by our team member.
- `bedroom-edited.webp`: our edit for the game. We removed the Moodlings hidden in the furniture, the scratch marks, the motion lines and the cat at the window, because in the story the feelings have left the room and Shwe is out at night.
- In the game (`frontend/assets/home/`) the room starts grey (`room-grey.webp`). Each feeling the player brings home gives the colour back to one part of the room (`room.webp`): bed, curtains, photos, rug and books, toys, desk, lights, plants, and the night sky.
- Thiri and Shwe are drawn in code on top of the picture.
