# Lakeside map: process

Map for **Night 6 · Lakeside** (4:30 AM) and **Night 7 · Sunrise**, the end of the story. Drawn by `drawLakeSky()` and `drawLakeWorld()` in `game/js/game.js`. There is no picture file: everything is painted in code, so it stays sharp on any screen.

Made on 17 Oct 2026 with Claude (Anthropic), following the team's design brief (Lakeside at dawn, green and pink).

| File | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `1-night6-lakeside.png` | Night 6: purple pre-dawn sky, stars still out |
| `2-night7-start.png` | Night 7 at the start: sky beginning to warm |
| `3-night7-sunrise.png` | Night 7 later: the sun has risen over the mountains |

## Layers
1. **Sky:** gradient that warms from night purple to pink and gold; stars fade out; on Night 7 the sun rises as the timer runs down (the night literally ends at sunrise).
2. **Mountains:** two ranges moving at different speeds.
3. **Lake:** pink-to-blue water, the sun's shimmering reflection, ripples, far-shore pines, a bobbing rowboat.
4. **Shore (front):** grass edge, sandy path, swaying reed beds with cattails (where Calm hides), wooden jetties on posts (the platforms).
