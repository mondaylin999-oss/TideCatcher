## Original work
- **The music is original**: composed by code from chord and pentatonic-scale rules with a fixed random seed, played by synthesized piano, music box, bells and pads. No samples, no existing melody. Because it uses common scales, a chance resemblance to some tune is possible; I did not check against any database.
- The pounce swoosh/landing, the scratch swipe, and all other effects and environments were generated by the scripts in `tools/`. The scripts were written with an AI assistant (Claude); declare that if your rules require it. No AI audio model was used.
