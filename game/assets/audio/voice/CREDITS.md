# Voice acting (intro and morning ending)

One clip per line of the intro (`intro/vo_XX.mp3`) and the morning ending (`ending/vo_XX.mp3`), numbered like the story lines in `game.js`. Lines with only a sound effect (the paper tearing, Shwe's plain meow) have no clip. `reactions/` holds Thiri's laugh and Shy's giggle.

| Asset | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Thiri, narrator and Moodling voices | Generated with the Kokoro-82M text-to-speech model (Apache-2.0) through kokoro-onnx (MIT). The voices are blends of Kokoro's built-in voice packs, not a copy of any real person. | Apache-2.0 allows commercial use of the output. |
| Laugh and giggle | Synthesised from scratch (shaped noise and tone). | Original to this project. |
